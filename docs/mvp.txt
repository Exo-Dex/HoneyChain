# 14. Our MVP definition

I'd formally freeze the scope as:

> ### **Honey Chain MVP**
>
> A prototype that allows a verified beekeeper to monitor a hive using IoT/simulated data, receive AI-assisted hive insights, record a honey harvest, create a traceable batch, record its subsequent processing and quality events, anchor those events to a blockchain, and allow a consumer to scan a QR code to verify the batch's provenance.

That's a **very achievable MVP**, while still satisfying the spirit of every major requirement in PS 26021.

And the research we've already done gives us a very solid basis for it: Madhukranti provides the precedent for beekeeper/honey-chain registration, KVIC Honey Mission provides the beneficiary/beekeeping context, FSSAI provides the quality-data vocabulary, and GS1's event-based traceability model gives us a defensible way to structure the batch journey.

**So from this point, I would treat "full Honey Chain" as the future roadmap and "this vertical slice" as Team Odysseus' actual current product.**
